Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon white sugar
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tablespoons butter, melted

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar.
  2. In another bowl, whisk together the milk and egg.
  3. Melt the butter in a small saucepan over low heat.
  4. Gradually add the melted butter to the milk and egg mixture, stirring to combine.
  5. Slowly pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients, stirring until just combined.
  6. Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium-high heat.
  7. Pour 1/4 cup batter for each pancake onto the griddle.
  8. Cook until bubbles form on the surface, then flip and cook until golden brown.
